# Preserve an original 2Brain identity within the Notion-backed system

2Brain follows Notion-backed layout, interaction, density, typography, spacing, color, state, and motion patterns while retaining an original 2Brain workspace icon, wordmark, terminology, and data. Notion trademarks, logos, illustrations, and proprietary assets are excluded; open-source icons may be selected and sized to match the referenced interaction pattern without presenting 2Brain as Notion.
